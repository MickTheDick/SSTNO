# SSTNO proposed setting

**Status: draft only. Not implemented. Not a verified TNO source.**

Made by MickTheDick. Proudly made by AI.

This file is a proposed setting for a later SSTNO map. Nothing in it is playable except where the developer baseline is named on purpose. The background below was taken from a teammate draft and was not checked against a current TNO build. Names and wars that the draft attributes to TNO are labeled as proposed borrowings. Towns, strongholds, mottos, and local jobs are SSTNO inventions. Either can change.

The playable build is still the grass developer pad in [Docs/SSTNO/CONTENT_CLEANUP.md](Docs/SSTNO/CONTENT_CLEANUP.md): one human Civilian, no faction bases, and no regional map. Station, shuttle, species, and antagonist files are excluded from the normal round. They were not deleted.

## Proposed year

The draft sets the story in 1962, after an Axis victory and a failed reunification war in western Russia. In that draft, Germany won the Second World War, the Soviet Union broke apart, Marshal Yegorov's West Russian Revolutionary Front struck west in 1956, and Samara fell in 1958 after collaborators and monarchists turned on both Berlin and the Reds. The draft calls the aftermath the Smuta.

Treat that paragraph as proposed TNO-inspired background, not as a citation. SSTNO has not confirmed those dates or leaders against the mod.

## Proposed borderland

The draft places the server on a fictional edge between claims, called here the Kama–Volga borderlands. These places do not exist in the build:

- A neutral town: market, clinic, workshops, granary, and a rail siding.
- An outlying works: a poor farm or ruined factory that the draft treats as contested.
- Roads between the settlements, which the draft treats as the dangerous supply routes.
- Four faction strongholds, one for each group below.

Civilians keeping the region alive, and soldiers keeping it divided, is the target design. It is not the current round.

## Proposed factions

These are plans. There is no faction selection, stronghold, or motto in game.

### The Tsar's Men

Draft borrowing: Vyatka and the ROVS monarchists, described as White émigrés who returned with German help and later turned on them, serving a tsar the draft names Vladimir III.

SSTNO invention: a fortified monastery estate in the north, and the motto "For Faith, Tsar and Fatherland."

### The Liberation Army

Draft borrowing: Samara and Andrey Vlasov's ROA, described as a force other factions treat as traitors.

SSTNO invention: a southern rail depot built around a dead armored train, and the motto "Neither Hitler nor Stalin."

### The Red Partisans

Draft borrowing: remnants of the West Russian Revolutionary Front, with late orders from Arkhangelsk and the draft's mention of Zhukov and Tukhachevsky.

SSTNO invention: forest bunkers, raids on the local roads, and the motto below.

> "The Front has not fallen while one of us still fights."
>
> SSTNO fictional flavor, written for this fork as if spoken by a Red partisan in the Kama forests in 1962. It is not a historical quotation and it is not a line from TNO.

### The Tatar Defense Committee

Draft borrowing: a Tatar national claim that appears when the Front collapses. The draft does not cite a TNO source page for the local committee.

SSTNO invention: a fortified village around an old mosque, trade with anyone, and the motto "This is our land. You are guests on it."

### Later threats in the draft

Not scheduled and not implemented: raiders the draft calls the Aryan Brotherhood from Perm, a worn German patrol from Reichskommissariat Moskowien, and missionaries it calls the Order of Saint George.

## Proposed civilian work

The draft suggests these local jobs: starosta, feldsher, mechanic, farmer, granary keeper, speculator, priest or mullah, railwayman, teacher, and radio operator. They are not character roles. The build's only SSTNO job is Civilian on the developer pad.
